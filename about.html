Stories bring us together.

North Pier Books began with a secondhand shelf and a belief that a neighborhood needs room for a good conversation.

Today, our shelves bring together fiction, nature writing, thoughtful design, and the books our neighbors keep recommending.

What you will find here

  • A carefully chosen shelf of independent titles.
  • A quiet place to sit and read a few pages.
  • People who are happy to help you find your next book.
